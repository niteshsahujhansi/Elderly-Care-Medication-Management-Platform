💡 My Project:
CareCircle — A Smart, Interconnected Elderly Care & Healthcare Routine Platform designed to empower seniors with independence while giving families and caregivers complete peace of mind.

🔍 Problem Statement:
Elderly Care & Medication Management Platform

🛠️ What I Built in 7 Hours:
CareCircle addresses elderly medication non-adherence, clinical confusion, and family anxiety through an empathetic web platform:

• 🔐 Passcode Care Ecosystem: Frictionless 6-digit PIN login connecting Seniors (Kiosk mode), Family Members, Visiting Nurses, and Viewers via a shared Universal Circle Code.
• 💊 4-Slot Routine Checklist: Chronological timetable (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Bedtime) with 1-tap dose logging ("I Took This", "Skip with Note", "Undo") & 7-Day adherence streaks.
• 🚨 India Emergency SOS: 1-tap dialing for National Emergency (112), Ambulance (108), and Senior ElderLine (14567), plus 1-click Care Circle SOS broadcast & on-screen paramedic medical summary.
• 📧 Proactive Email Alerts: Automated background engine scanning for overdue doses (>30m) & clinic visits, dispatching proactive email reminders.
• 🌐 100% Hindi/English Bilingual Mode: 1-click header switcher making the entire platform accessible to diverse elderly households across India.
• ✨ AI Companion & Wellness Hub: Daily emoji mood check-ins with empathetic AI responses, regular exercises & hydration tracker, daily offline AI digest, and call ratings.

💻 Tech Stack:
• Frontend: Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
• Backend: FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Pydantic, SQLite / PostgreSQL
• Testing: Pytest (21/21 passing in 0.71s), TypeScript (0 errors)
• Deployment: Vercel (Frontend) + Render (Backend) + GitHub CI
